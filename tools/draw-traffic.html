Traffic zones and bike racks

Paint the crowded paths, or add the bike racks that you see

Loading the streets…

  1. Pick High or Medium.
  2. Click Paint. Then press and drag over the busy streets. The streets under the paint change color.
  3. Click Move map to pan. The mouse wheel zooms in all modes.
  4. Each new stroke is a new zone. Click Paint more on a zone to add strokes to it.
  5. The app makes all zones busy in the 10 minutes between classes.
  6. Only the streets that change color count. Paint on a path that is not in the street graph has no effect.

    1. Tap the map where a rack is. Zoom in close first.
    2. Or stand next to the rack and click Add at my location.
    3. Drag a gold number to move a rack. Then fill in the details.
    4. The gray dots are the racks from the UCSD campus map and OpenStreetMap. The app has them already.
