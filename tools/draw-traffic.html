Draw traffic zones

Paint over the streets that are busy at class change

Loading the streets…

  1. Pick High or Medium.
  2. Click Paint. Then press and drag over the busy streets. The streets under the paint change color.
  3. Click Move map to pan. The mouse wheel zooms in all modes.
  4. Each new stroke is a new zone. Click Paint more on a zone to add strokes to it.
  5. Type the times in each zone, or tell Claude the times by zone number.

    The browser keeps your drawing for the next visit. Send the file to Claude, or put it in the data/ folder of the project.
